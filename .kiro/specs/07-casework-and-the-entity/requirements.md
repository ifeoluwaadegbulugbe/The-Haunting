# Spec 07 — Casework and the entity: requirements

## Goal

Borrow what makes hidden-object detective games compulsive (a steady stream of small wins, chapters, a suspect closing in, a dramatic arrest) and go beyond them with an entity that reaches the player directly. "AI writes the words, code decides the facts" is unchanged. The 100-point score is unchanged.

## Requirements

### R1 Room searches
1. Timed hidden-object searches of the study and Lily's room: eight listed items, combo scoring for quick finds, a penalty for wrong clicks, 1 to 5 stars.
2. Four modes, each opened by a star in the one before: normal, by candlelight, scrambled names, and 11:13 (objects the entity has moved).
3. A free hint every 30 seconds. Stars are spent on lab speed-ups and on Archivist Reed's specific hints.

### R2 Forensics lab
1. Five tasks (truth file §9.12), each opened by the evidence it needs: dusting, a jigsaw, a pencil rubbing, a UV sweep and a plate check.
2. Three go to the lab and return after a real-time wait that one star skips.
3. A killer profile of six traits fills in from evidence, facts and forensics; each suspect shows how many known traits fit.

### R3 Chapters and a partner
1. Three chapters close as the case's sections unlock, each with a cliffhanger card shown once.
2. Archivist Reed comments in text at fixed moments, never blocking play.

### R4 The confrontation
1. Naming Julian leads to five defence points, each answered with evidence from a fixed table. Wrong answers count toward a trial grade only. A guilty verdict follows.

### R5 The entity
1. It can be caught for a moment in the HQ mirror, between the study books, at Lily's window and in the interview room doorway. Each catch reveals one of six memories of Clara's last hour, kept in the notebook.
2. It renames the tab while the player is away, changes the family photograph when they return, calls at 11:13 on the player's own clock, and blows out the study candles while hostile.
3. It remembers how it was treated, and the cellar ending changes with it. None of this changes evidence, facts, unlocks or the score.

## Out of scope
- Voice. Generated art (the API key has no image quota; existing art and drawn fallbacks are used).
